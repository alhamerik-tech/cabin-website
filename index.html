How Wasel works

A clear path from paperwork to follow-up

Wasel assists with preparing, typing, and following up your transactions. Final approvals and issuance remain with the relevant UAE government authorities.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you need

    Choose a service, share your details, and upload your documents through the request form.

  2. 02

    We prepare & type

    Our team reviews your paperwork, types the required forms, and prepares your transaction for submission.

  3. 03

    We follow up

    We track progress and keep you updated at every stage using your WSL reference number.

  4. 04

    You stay informed

    Approvals and issuance are handled by the relevant UAE authorities — we make sure you always know where things stand.
